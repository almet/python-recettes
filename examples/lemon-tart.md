# Preparation

## Step 1

Beat {{ 75g of sugar }} with {{ 2 whole eggs }} and {{ 3 egg yolks }}.

## Step 2

Add {{ 200ml of lemon juice }}, some {{ lemon zest }}, {{ 30g of melted butter }}, and {{ 25g of cornstarch }} diluted in a little water. Bring to the boil, stirring constantly, until it thickens.

## Step 3

Crush {{ 200g of speculoos biscuits }} and mix them with {{ 75g of melted butter }}.
Spread over the bottom of a 20-25cm {{ tart dish }} and refrigerate for 30min.

## Step 4

Make a meringue with the {{ 3 egg whites }} and {{ 1 pinch of salt }}.

Beat the whites to stiff peaks and gradually add {{ 150g of icing sugar }}.

## Step 5

Assemble.

## Step 6

Put the tart under the oven grill for a few minutes, or brown it with a blowtorch.
