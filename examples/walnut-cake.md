From Mamie Lucette.

# Preparation

Preheat the oven to *180ºC*.

## Step 1

Blend {{ 250g of walnuts }}.

## Step 2

Beat the {{ 6 egg yolks }} with {{ 200g of sugar }}. Stir in the walnuts and {{ 50g of breadcrumbs }}.

## Step 3

Gently fold in {{ 6 egg whites }} beaten to stiff peaks.

## Step 4

Bake for 30min.
