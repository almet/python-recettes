# Preparation

## Step 1

Preheat the oven to *200ºC*.

## Step 2

Brown {{ 115g of butter }} in a pan (6-7min).

## Step 3

In a first bowl, mix {{ 160g of flour }}, {{ a pinch of salt }}, and {{ 1/2 tsp of baking soda }}.

## Step 4

Wait for the butter to cool down.

In a second bowl, mix {{ 70g of white sugar }} with {{ 80g of brown sugar }}, and stir in the butter.

Add {{ 1 egg }} and {{ 1 tsp of vanilla extract }}, and mix well.

## Step 5

Combine both bowls without overmixing, to keep some texture, then add the {{ chocolate chips }}.

## Step 6

Bake for 8 to 10min.
